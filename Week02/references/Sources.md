## Planning assumptions

- ABC Startup Solutions, its specific address and its technology environment are fictional for this academic scenario.
- Device quantities, subnet assignments, ISP bandwidth targets, security policies and recovery objectives are proposed engineering decisions, not vendor specifications or deployment evidence.
- Microsoft 365 Business Premium includes the Microsoft 365 Apps, Intune, Entra ID P1 and Microsoft Defender for Business referenced in the plan; licensing should be checked at procurement.
- A paid AnyDesk plan is necessary for commercial support initiation.
- Physical floor measurement, Wi-Fi survey, cabling quote, IPS/NGFW throughput assessment, ISP serviceability and budget validation are required before purchasing.
